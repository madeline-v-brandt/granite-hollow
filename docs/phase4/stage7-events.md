# Phase 4, Stage 7: events and stories

Status: **built and reviewed (2026-11-27):** approved after the changes in "After the first look". The plan is the Phase 4 brief's events stage, with `stage0-review.md` §6 (the seasonal events) and the decisions in §5:
- each event's day always dry
- the fall comp as decided
- smaller things are stories, not events

After the first look (below) there are two comps a year, and Trail Day is in summer.

Granite Hollow now has a calendar. Five times a year everyone turns up for something, and in between, the simulation's own goings-on (grade arguments, big new problems) become things people talk about.

## The five events

Each on a fixed date:

| Season | Event | Date | The day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall | **The Granite Hollow Bouldering Comp** | November 1 | Your five best sends of the day score. Results and a podium at dusk. |
| Winter | **The Solstice Bonfire** | December 21 | Crisp friction while it's light (the day is always Prime), then everyone round the fire at The Tailgate at dusk, with stories from Gus and the Bishop |
| Spring | **The Granite Hollow Spring Comp** | April 1 | Scored the same way as the fall comp. Gus says it isn't an April Fool. |
| Summer | **The Midsummer Dusk Session** | June 21 | Everyone climbs until the light goes, then headlamps and Otis's sandwiches |
| Summer | **Trail Day** | the first Saturday in August | The volunteers reopen one of the old trails (below). Help Gus for two hours and lines you've found get brushed. |

For every event:
- **The day is always dry,** at least Good. If a save's forecast already said rain for it, that's corrected.
- **Every regular comes out for it,** whatever their routine, and the Bishop turns up too. Busier than usual, so you meet more strangers.
- **You can't miss it by accident:**
  - **Before:** it's flagged in gold on the forecast, with "★ Coming up" under the forecast when it's further out. Gus puts a poster up at the kiosk, which shows in the day's opening text, Local Buzz and the Here tab, a week or so ahead.
  - **On the day:** the day's opening says what's on, and a card in the Here tab says what to do.
- **Missing it costs nothing.** It happens without you: Local Buzz says how it went, and people talk about it afterwards.
- **Being there together** counts towards friendship with every regular you know who's there (a new `event` moment).

## The comps

The fall comp is as decided in Stage 0, and the spring comp works the same way:
- **Every problem in the park counts,** repeats included.
- **No divisions.**
- **Your five best sends score.** Each line counts once, at 100 a V-grade, plus 50 for a flash.

During the day:
- **The Here tab** has your card: your five scoring sends and your total. Under it are the **leaders so far**, which fill in as the others' sends happen through the day.
- **After each send,** the result says your comp total.

**At dusk,** before the day's summary:
- Gus climbs onto the picnic table with his clipboard and reads out the results.
- A small illustrated podium (first in the middle, arms up) shows the top three in their own colours, with Pickles sitting in front, very proud of everybody.
- If you'd already gone home, Gus texts the results round instead.

**What you keep:**
- your place in the journal
- "at the comp" next to that day's sends in My logbook
- a ribbon on the Climber page if you're in the top three (gold, silver or bronze), under **Ribbons and gatherings**, which also lists every event you've been to

**The field** (the same rules for both comps):
- **Who's in it:** the regulars who climb (Gus runs it), plus up to nine of the community's strongest who've arrived.
- **How they score:** each one's five sends come from their level that day, a little below their best, with some luck. Nobody climbs harder than the hardest line established by then.
- **Fixed per save:** it's worked out from the save's own random stream, so it's the same on every reload and changes nothing else.

**Beating Dez.** Dez gets a little nervous at comps (as Dez admits afterwards), so Dez climbs about half a grade below Dez's best on the day. In simulated crags:

| | Dez scores | Third place | What it takes |
|---|---|---|---|
| First comp (Nov 2025) | about 5,450–5,650 | about 3,400–4,200 | A good player around V7 is near the podium |
| First spring comp (Apr 2026) | about 5,700–5,900 | | |
| Second fall comp (Nov 2026) | about 5,700–6,000 | | About five V12s in a day beats Dez: possible for a strong climber |

**Afterwards:** for a fortnight, the regulars talk about it:
- Gus: "I read your name out twice, I was that pleased. Pickles barked both times."
- Hollis checked Gus's arithmetic twice.
- Dez: "Won the comp. …Don't tell anyone I was nervous."
- Bea, if you beat her: "Rematch. Next year. I'm already training."

## The Solstice Bonfire and the Midsummer Dusk Session

**Stay until dusk** (or head home within half an hour of it) and you're there. A small scene follows:
- **The bonfire:**
  - an illustration of everyone round the fire
  - who's come along
  - one of Gus's stories (the frozen quarry pond in '96, the teenager with a bath towel for a crash pad, how Pickles turned up)
  - one of the Bishop's, which is unreliable (the first ascent of The Billboard "in 1974, or 1977… at some point there was a mule"; Hollis takes notes on what's changed since last time)
- **The dusk session:** headlamps coming on across the boulders "like slow fireflies", Otis's sandwiches, and the last problem going by the light of three headlamps and a phone.

**Small touches:**
- If you're with Rowan, Rowan shares your blanket at the bonfire, or draws the headlamps.
- If Pickles is yours, he falls asleep with his nose nearly in the fire, or chases moths through the beams.

## Trail Day and the old trails

The trails all branch out from The Tailgate, so between two neighbouring areas you often walk back through the hub. **Four old paths** from the quarry and mill days join areas the trails don't:

| Opens | Path | Joins | A trip between them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aug 1, 2026 | the Quarrymen's Path | Hawkwatch and Millrace Caves | 14 min, about 6 with the path |
| Aug 7, 2027 | the old Cart Track | Bracken Gully and The Grout Piles | 21 min, about 10 |
| Aug 5, 2028 | the Wall Path | Back Forty and The Grout Piles | 16 min, about 11 |
| Aug 4, 2029 | the Miller's Path | The Spillway and Bracken Gully | 16 min, about 8 |

How they work:
- **Before:** they're on the map as faint, dotted, overgrown lines through the woods, and the trees keep off them.
- **Each Trail Day** the volunteers reopen the next one. The day's opening says which.
- **From that morning,** it's drawn as a proper trail on the map and the trail map, and walks use it: trips the game walks for you, and walking yourself at trail pace.
- **It opens whether or not you help.** If you missed the day, Local Buzz says it's open.

**Helping:** a card in the Here tab, **Help with Trail Day · 2 hours**. It costs a little energy, and:
- **Lines brushed:** up to six dirty lines you've found are brushed clean, nearest the lot first, ready to try.
- **The walk:** Gus walks you the length of the new trail ("Saves going round by the lot," he says, very pleased with everybody).
- **Friendship:** Gus counts it as looking after the park, which he values, and everyone there counts it as time together.

From 2030 there are no more old paths to reopen, and Trail Day is about cutting back the summer's growth.

## Stories from the simulation

Nothing new is invented: these come from what the save's community has actually done.
- **Grade disputes.** When a regular repeats a line and thinks it's a grade off (the community's logs already record this), Local Buzz says so, for example: "Hollis reckons Pilgrim's Crack (V5) is sandbagged: more like V6. Dez, who put it up, does not agree." The people involved bring it up:
  - Hollis files it under "Disputes, ongoing"
  - Rowan, whose problem it is: "I'm choosing to take it as a compliment"
  - the Bishop: "In '77 we had two grades: 'hard' and 'Dot did it'."
- **Celebrations.** When a regular puts up a big new problem (V8 or harder), everyone else mentions the party:
  - Gus: "half the Hollow turned up at the lot to celebrate. I pretended not to enjoy it."
  - Bea: "There was CAKE. Well, Otis had a muffin."
- **Busy days.** On a dry, Prime weekend: "Word's out that it's prime: the lot's busier than usual." You meet more of the community that day.
- **Already there** from earlier stages: the regulars' long projects going down, the first problem in a new area, a newly named area, a regular's arrival.

**As before,** everything in Local Buzz and conversation goes through what you know: a line or boulder you haven't found is described, never named.

## Saves

All new fields are optional and cleaned on load:
- **`G.events[date]`:** what you did at each event.
  - a comp: `score`, `place`, `of`, `there`
  - Trail Day: `helped`, `trail` (which old trail)
  - the gatherings: `there`
  - A record whose date isn't that event's day is dropped.
- **`G.day.trail`** (you helped today) and **`G.day.busy`** (a busy prime weekend).
- **Not stored:** the calendar, the comp's field and which old trails are open. They're worked out from the date and the save's world.
